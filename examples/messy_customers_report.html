What was decided

  • Target: upgraded (classification, scored by roc_auc)
  • One row: one row = one customer ref
  • Split: Trained on the earliest 1,920 rows by signup, tested on the latest 480.
  • Duplicates: exact copy rows were dropped before anything else.
  • Kept out: customer_ref

What the data showed

Most informative on their own: visits_30d, segment, signup_month, support_tickets, income_band_usd, pages_per_visit.

  • income has a long tail (skew 14.1; 3.8% of values are extreme). A few huge values pull linear models around; trees do not mind. → No log version added: it is being switched off as a near-copy.
  • income_band_usd has a long tail (skew 14.1; 3.8% of values are extreme). A few huge values pull linear models around; trees do not mind. → Added income_band_usd__log, the log of it, which spreads the values out.
  • visits_30d has a long tail (skew 2.1; 0.8% of values are extreme). A few huge values pull linear models around; trees do not mind. → Added visits_30d__log, the log of it, which spreads the values out.
  • income, income_band_usd move together almost perfectly (rank correlation 1.00). One is enough. → Keeping income_band_usd, the one most linked to the target; suggesting income off.
  • In region, the same label is written several ways ('North ' / 'north') and 'unknown' on 16% of rows looks like a placeholder for 'not known' rather than a real value. → Added region__clean (trimmed, lower case, placeholders as missing) and switched region off.
  • pages_per_visit is missing on 15% of rows, and that is itself a clue: the outcome rate is 10% when it is missing against 27% when not. → Kept as a signal: training adds a 'was missing' flag next to the filled-in value.
  • discount_pct is missing on 29% of rows, and that is itself a clue: the outcome rate is 19% when it is missing against 27% when not. → Kept as a signal: training adds a 'was missing' flag next to the filled-in value.

Risks and open concerns

  • Much better on the rows it trained on (0.960) than on unseen rows (0.820). It is partly memorising.
  • Not clearly better than linear, boosting, tuned_forest, tuned_boosting, tuned_linear, ensemble: resampling the 480 unseen rows, its lead could be zero. Treat them as tied and prefer the simpler or more readable one.
